01THE JOURNEY ON · 2025

The Journey On

A time has come,
a time has gone,
and still another waits beyond the dawn.

For it is in the journey
that growth takes root—
along the winding road
I have walked for many seasons.

Yet there are things
I must release to travel farther.
Old comforts cling like familiar shadows,
and though I long to leave them behind,
they whisper of safety
in a voice I know too well.

But what is there to fear?

Death comes for all.
Hardship visits every door.
And joy, too, finds its way to every heart.

Why tremble before the tide
that carries all things in and out?
Why fear what is certain?

No wall can shield us
from the turning of life's seasons.

Happiness cannot be the aim,
for it drifts like mist at morning
and leaves no roots behind.

Seek instead what endures.

Joy that is deeper than circumstance.
Peace that remains when storms arise.

Fix your eyes upon these.

Endure.

Find joy in enduring.
Find peace in enduring.

It can be done.

For fear and darkness bear no fruit.
They leave the hands empty
and the spirit diminished.

Stand firm.
Be steadfast.
Take courage.

Be content.
Do not hunger endlessly for more.

For we possess, even now,
all that is needed for this day—

and that is enough.

02GOLDEN · 2025

Golden

How could I long for such a simple thing?
The world is ours for the taking, every
fleeting thought, every desire
There are no limits yet we covet and yearn
for things we can't have
The chaos of this world is distracting
Turning us into mindless husks
Is there no escape? Is there no reprieve?
We never realized what beauty we have
Never realized it's golden

03THE GIFT WE SQUANDER · 2025

The Gift We Squander

From the abyss we come,
longing to feel anything.
What can we offer, but to exist?
We are but dust in the wind,
no more sacred than the dirt itself.

We pine for acceptance,
like a hand reaching for smoke,
yet crave freedom.
It was never ours to have,
but a gift we squander.

We search, we beg—
anything to feel real.

The warmth of the Son
on a brisk morning.
Our gaze is upon the sunrise;
life churns within our bones.
We begin to accept our being.

The moment is brief—
distractions take hold.
We are not worthy.
There is no reprieve,
there is no closure.
Into the depths we return.

04TIME AND TIME AGAIN · 2024

Time and Time Again

Time and time again
Thrashed upon the cliffs
Time and time again
The waves tear asunder
Too long he has self-inflicted
Today, he says, will be different
Time and time again
Traversing through new terrain
To only find his way back
Time and time again….

05ROSE · 2023

Rose

A rose, beautiful, yet deceiving
Color of boldness and confidence
So beautiful am I
Is this what I've become?
This husk of pretentious appearances
A deep look inside
The truth inside devours me whole
I can never escape

06WITHOUT YOU · 2025

Without You

Don't be far away, Don't forget me
My heart is fragile, On the precipice of implosion
For you, my love, I would rip it out
Bloody your hands
Sink my teeth into death itself
For there is no life
Without you

07I AM WORN · 2022

I Am Worn

I am worn
I am spread thin
My soul feels leeched
Of the life it once held proudly
The strong lengthy fingers of apathy
Gripping the throat asphyxiation is imminent
Perhaps there is no worry amongst the dead
No pain or sufferings to experience
Sometimes I long for this reality
Once more I am faint
How will I endure
I am worn

Take Heart

What is love? What do we care?
I can't get over the gloom of my reality.
My nemesis controls me daily
Am I the problem? no
Am I the solution? yes
Take heart brethren, goodness is still to come
